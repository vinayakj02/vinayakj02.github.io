## KubeDay

In 2023, the primary CNCF-supported events in Bangalore focused on community-driven gatherings. Key events included KubeDay India (Dec 8), Kubernetes Community Days (KCD) Bengaluru (June 2), and various specialized meetups focusing on Nephio, security, and sustainability.
