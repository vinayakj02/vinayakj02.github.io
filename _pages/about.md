I am a software developer and a tinkerer, currently working as a Software Engineer II at [Visa Inc](https://www.visa.co.in/), where I spend my days working with containers, databases, computer networking, and backend development.

I graduated from the National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK) with a major in Information Technology. During my time there, I was Systems Lead at [IRIS, NITK](https://iris.nitk.ac.in/about_us), a student tech team, where I got to tinker with a lot of the infrastructure that keeps the institute running. Along the way, I interned at [Visa Inc](https://www.visa.co.in/), [Futurewei Technologies](https://www.futurewei.com/), and [Fourth Frontier](https://fourthfrontier.com/), learning how the other half runs their systems.

I also volunteered with the [Web Enthusiasts' Club](https://webclub.nitk.ac.in/) and the Computer Society of [IEEE, NITK](https://ieee.nitk.ac.in/), where I got to interact with a lot of my friends in tech and beyond.

My interests are broad, including but not limited to containers, databases, networking, and backend systems. I like understanding how things work under the hood, and I keep tinkering because the discovery is the fun part.

When I am not working, I am [speedcubing](https://x3.vinayakj02.com), tinkering with my hobby projects, or on the guitar and piano.
